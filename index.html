About

A dancer behind the decks

Yindra reads a room like a dancer, because he is one. Born in Barcelona with Ibizan and African roots, he spent his first decade on stage with urban and contemporary dance companies, touring Spain, Germany, France, Italy and England.

Then came the booth. As Will Fly he played after-parties at Raum, plus Bloc, City Hall and Razzmatazz, and was on the Piknic Électronik bill with Nina Kraviz. In Ibiza he has played Café del Mar and Guaraná.

Two sides, one dancer: hip hop and R&B power mixes for the party, deep house, afro and techno for the club, and meditative downtempo for sunsets and slow rooms.
